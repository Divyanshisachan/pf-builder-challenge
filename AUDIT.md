## What the audit shows (30 PF listings)

| Signal | Count | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Listed by holiday-home / vacation-rental operators (DTCM permit) | **29/30** (+1 hotel) | "Monthly rent" here is mostly short-stay hospitality with hotel-style fees, which isn't obvious from the card |
| "X% less than average" badge compares monthly rent with an annual average | **29/30** | The trust signal itself misleads; like-for-like, most are **12–35% above** average |
| Broker licence is a placeholder (00000, na, -, 00) | **20/30** | The regulatory field looks filled but proves nothing |
| No permit shown, or permit = "tmp" | **4/30** | #8, #12, #23, #26 |
| Description says whether photos are real or indicative | **0/30** | Only Bayut and Dubizzle disclose "illustrative" |
| Chiller cost stated | **6/30** | Common hidden cost in Dubai |
| VAT stated | 13/30 | |
| All-in can be computed from the listing alone | **0/30** | Every listing leaves at least one cost unpriced |
| Stated extras push the price **> 7,500** although the headline is ≤ 7,500 | 6 (#6, #7, #9, #10, #11, #13) | Budget breaks after the click |
| Listed 6+ months ago | **14/30** | Likely stale or "evergreen" bait |
| Same unit listed twice / recycled photo set / shared permit | #6=#7 · #2/#6/#7 photos · #1/#2 permit | One "choice" is really one unit |
| Same building + size, different price | #14 vs #28 (7,000 vs 7,999) | Searcher can't see it side by side |
| User would reach out now | **12/32** (#10, #14–#21, #24, #25, #32) | Driven by taste + clear description + review volume |

**Bottom line:** no listing is clearly fake, but **none is fully trustworthy on its own**. The searcher can't know the real price, whether the photos show the unit, or whether it's still available, without messaging the agent, and that message leaves Property Finder.
