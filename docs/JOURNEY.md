# User journey: Divyanshi (short-term renter, searching from India)

As described by the user. Pain points marked ⚠️.

## 1. Start
- Downloads the Property Finder app on her phone.
- Logs in with her **Indian phone number**, from India.
- PF starts showing a bunch of listings.

## 2. Search and shortlist
- Applies filters to match her requirements.
- Goes through the listings one by one and shortlists a few.

## 3. Contact the agent
- Two options: **Call** or **WhatsApp**.
- ⚠️ Calling needs international calling enabled, which is a hassle.
- ⚠️ Dubai agents usually don't pick up foreign numbers (they think it's a scam).
- So: **WhatsApp**. PF opens WhatsApp with an automated message containing the listing ("the agent will contact you"). From there, WhatsApp runs on its own, outside PF.

## 4. Agent responds, or doesn't
- **A. No response:** ⚠️ the listing she shortlisted and was interested in is simply lost.
- **B. Agent responds:** ⚠️ the agent has no information about what she's looking for, so she **explains everything from scratch** (who she is, what she needs).

## 5. Budget check with the agent
- **In budget:** the agent sends videos and photos. If she likes them, she'd want to visit, but ⚠️ she **can't book without visiting**, and she isn't in Dubai yet. She finalises the numbers, then shortlists it.
- **Not in budget:** a good agent offers **other properties in her budget** from their own listings. Reaching out to good agents pays off.

## 6. Ongoing pains
- ⚠️ **Loses track of listings** across many agents and chats.
- ⚠️ **Peak season:** listings get taken by others, so she has to decide very fast.
- ⚠️ **Remote:** she can't visit to finalise while in India.

## 7. How a remote deal has actually closed
- Once, she finalised **purely from the listing images on WhatsApp plus the agency's reputation**, without visiting.
