# Findings log (user's own observations, verbatim intent)

## Steps 1–2: Scout + evidence (Rent)

1. **Photos show surroundings, not the unit.** In the first listings reviewed, few images show the actual room or building amenities; most show surrounding scenery/skyline to look luxurious or show location. The searcher can't see what they'd actually live in.
2. **Recycled / stock unit photos.** Same apartment photos appear across many listings; when contacted, agents send different videos/photos of the real unit. Listings don't always disclose that photos are indicative. (Listing #2)
3. **Missing trust signals.** Building name not stated in listing; agent has no ratings → hard to trust. (Listing #2)
4. **Same "area" photos reused across different buildings.** Surrounding-area images in the last 2–3 listings look identical, as if pulled from Google, and don't match the feel of the actual units. (Listings #1–3)
5. **Photos can't prove location.** Even plausible unit photos don't show the view from window/balcony, so the searcher can't confirm the unit is in the claimed area. (Listing #3)
6. **Agents without ratings recur.** (Listings #2, #3)
7. **True monthly cost not stated.** Description doesn't say DEWA, chiller, tourism tax, VAT or other fees are included/excluded. Searcher must call the agent just to learn if it fits budget, and may only find out it doesn't after the call. Wants this listed upfront, or at least flagged as "ask the agent about this". (Listing #4)
8. **Price doesn't match what you get.** A plain-looking Marina studio is priced the same as a modern Business Bay one; more luxurious options exist at the same price. Searcher can't tell what justifies the price. (Listing #5)
9. **Floor and view not stated.** Matters for a searcher who wants a high floor with a view. (Listing #5)
10. **Inclusions stated = trust boost.** Listing #5 lists what is included/excluded, which the user called a good touch. (Listing #5)
11. **Same "bait" photo set across many agents.** One set of DAMAC Maison Privé interior photos appears on multiple listings (#2, #6, and the next). From past experience, ~95% of listings using these photos sent a different video/unit when contacted, and that look was never found in Business Bay. (Listing #6)
12. **Duplicate listing of the same unit.** #7 is the same unit as #6 (same price, size and photos) posted by a different agent (same agency, JSR Global). The searcher sees two "options" that are really one. (Listings #6, #7)
13. **Building/amenity photos add realism.** #7 adds corridor and amenity shots, making it feel more real than #6. (Listing #7)
14. **Generic community photos misrepresent the micro-location.** The same Business Bay community images appear on every listing; but Business Bay is large, and not every part has that view or is close to Downtown. Fine as "area flavour", misleading as "your view". (Listings #1–8)
15. **"Studio" that is really a hotel room.** #8 shows just a bed, no kitchen, yet it's listed as a studio at a high price (AED 6,499 for 334 sqft, the highest AED/sqft so far). For someone who cooks, the missing kitchen is a deal-breaker that the listing type hides. (Listing #8)
16. **Clear lease type builds confidence.** #9 says plainly that it's short-term only (daily/weekly/monthly, not annual). For a visitor this is exactly the signal needed, and it wasn't visible on earlier listings. (Listing #9)
17. **Disclosed fees build trust, but reveal the real price.** #9 lists tourism tax, VAT, service charge and a checkout/cleaning fee. The user liked it most, but once fees are added it's even further above budget. (Listing #9)
18. **What made the first "liked" listing:** enough real room photos + real studio with kitchen + good amenities + agent rated >4 + clear lease type + fees stated. (Listing #9)
