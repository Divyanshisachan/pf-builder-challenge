# Challenges and limitations (to carry into README and ONE-PAGER)

## 1. Could not try Scout (brief step: "Try it first")
- **What happened:** From India on 12 Nov 2026, Scout was not visible anywhere in the normal search on propertyfinder.ae (homepage, rent search) or in the app. The main search bar rejects plain English ("We can't find… check your spelling").
- Found it later at `propertyfinder.ae/scout/v2` ("PF Scout BETA"). After logging in: **"Due to high demand, access to Scout is currently limited. We've noted your interest and will get back to you."**
- **Result:** I have **not been able to try Scout**. My design is based on what Scout's login page promises, plus my own search experience, not on using Scout.
- **Evidence:** `evidence/scout/scout-login-wall-access-limited.png`, `evidence/scout/web-search-plain-english-fails.png`
- **What I'd need:** Scout beta access, to test how it handles a short-term renter's query and whether it surfaces fees, duplicates and stale listings.
- **Takeaway used in the design:** if a motivated user who is actively hunting for Scout can't reach it, a normal searcher won't. This is why the assistant must live **inside the search moments**, not on a separate page.
