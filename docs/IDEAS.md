# Feature ideas (user's brainstorm, raw)

1. **Visual taste picker in Scout.** Scout shows a set of apartment images; she taps the ones she likes (view, luxury, style); Scout learns her taste and returns listings that look like that. Origin: listing #18, liked for how it looks.
2. **Per-listing "hook" memory for agent chats.** Remember why she picked each listing, what to discuss, and the negotiation hook (e.g. "discounted for long-term"), available while chatting with each agent, on the go. Origin: findings #35–38.
3. **Surface long-stay offers without opening every listing.** Origin: finding #28.
4. **All-in cost calculator + shareable cost sheet** (VAT, service charge, tourism fee, deposit, agency fee) she can send to her manager for approval. Origin: findings #29, #50.
5. **Ask before showing.** Scout starts by understanding the persona (short-term renter / relocating long-term renter / buyer) and needs, then shows ~25 relevant listings instead of 100. Origin: findings #46–48.
