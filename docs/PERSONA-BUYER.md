# Persona: Rohan, the first-time buyer (end-user)

> "Tell me the real cash I need on day one, whether this price is fair against what people actually paid, and whether I can move in."

## Who he is
- 33, Indian, has worked in Dubai for 3 years; rents a 1BR in Business Bay (~AED 95k/yr)
- **First property purchase in the UAE**; wants to stop paying rent and live in it himself (not an investor)
- Comfortable with the area, **not with the buying process** (DLD, Oqood, title deed, mortgage, NOC)

## What he needs
- **Ready 1BR in Business Bay**, ~700–850 sqft, **vacant on transfer** (he needs to move in)
- Budget **≈ AED 1.5M**, with an **80% mortgage** (expat, first home)
- **Total upfront cash known before he calls anyone**: 20% down + DLD 4% + agency 2% (+VAT) + trustee + mortgage registration/valuation ≈ **AED 400k on 1.5M** (estimate)
- A **fair price vs actual DLD transactions** in the same building, not vs a vague "area average"
- Clear status: ready vs off-plan resale, tenanted vs vacant, service charge per sqft

## Goals
- Buy a fairly priced home he can move into within ~3–4 months
- No surprise cash requirement; mortgage pre-approval aligned to the right price
- One honest agent who answers the hard questions in writing

## Frustrations
- Listings say "ready" yet the handover is in the future; "1 bed" in the title, "studio" in the description
- "Tenanted": message us for the rent and lease end
- "Estimated upfront costs" link with no figures; fees never in the description
- Same unit posted by several agents; small-sample 5.0 agent ratings
- Off-plan resale vs ready isn't obvious from the card

## How he differs from the renter (Divyanshi)
| | Renter (Divyanshi) | Buyer (Rohan) |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden cost | Monthly: VAT, service charge, tourism fee, DEWA | One-off: DLD 4%, agency 2%, mortgage fees, ~27% of price in cash |
| Price truth | No rental comps on rent pages | DLD sale comps often shown, but not interpreted |
| Key risk | Bait photos, unit gone, fees after the click | Off-plan vs ready, tenanted vs vacant, overpaying vs comps |
| Timeline | Days (peak season) | Months (mortgage, NOC, transfer) |
| Agent role | Availability, real price, video | Comps, seller motivation, tenancy, viewing, offer |
