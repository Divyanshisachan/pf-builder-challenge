## What the table shows
- **#1 vs #3:** similar price (5,000 vs 5,500) and size (520 vs 600), but #1 discloses nothing while #3 discloses most items.
- **#4, #5 and #6:** almost the same listed price (6,990–6,999), yet the known minimum ranges from 6,990 to about 8,050 once stated extras are added.
- **AED/sqft ranges from 9.2 to 16.2** for studios in the same two areas; the cheapest per sqft (#1, #3) are the least or most transparent respectively.
- **#6 and #7 are the same unit** (same price, size, photos) from two agents of one agency.
- **No listing states chiller.** Only #6 states VAT. Only #2 gives a deposit amount.
