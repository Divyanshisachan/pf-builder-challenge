## How descriptions vary
- No two listings disclose the same set of costs. Each one covers a different subset (bills, tourism fee, commission, deposit) and stays silent on the rest.
- **Chiller, VAT, Ejari and photo authenticity are missing in all 4.**
- Only #2 states a deposit and its refund terms.
- Only #4 warns that the price can change (seasonal, holiday surcharges).
- #3 has an "Include All*" asterisk with no visible footnote, plus a "Limited Time Huge Discount" with no amount or end date.

## What a first-time renter would miss
1. **All 4 are holiday-home / vacation-rental operators (DTCM permits), not standard long-term rentals.** The search result doesn't make that obvious, and holiday homes can carry tourism fees and different terms.
2. **"Available from" dates are in the past** on #1, #2 and #3 (Mar 2025–Apr 2026), and those listings are 5–6 months old, so they may already be rented.
3. **#1 and #2 share the same agent AND the same DTCM permit number** but are in different areas (Marina vs Business Bay). One permit, two buildings.
4. **Broker licence is "00000" or "na"** on all 4, which looks like a placeholder.
5. **The "X% less than average" badge is misleading:** it compares a monthly price with an annual average (e.g. #2 at 6,000/mo = 72k/yr ≈ the 72,436 average, yet it is shown as "92% less").
6. **Size conflicts within the same listing** (#4: 500 vs 550 sqft).
7. **The true monthly cost can't be computed** from any single listing (DEWA + chiller + VAT + tourism fee + deposit + agency fee).
8. **Metro distance is never given.**
